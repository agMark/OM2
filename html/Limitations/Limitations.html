The Airworthiness Limitations Section is FAA approved and specifies maintenance required under 14 CFR §§ 43.16 and 91.403 of the Federal Aviation Regulations unless an alternative program has been FAA approved.

The FAA may require, in accordance with FAR 39, other inspections or repairs mandated by the issuance of airworthiness directives applicable to the airframe, engine, propeller, or appliance.

NOTE

You must review all applicable airworthiness limitations, some of which are not provided in this manual. Specifically, you must review the airworthiness limitations for the engine and propeller, and for any STC's that have been incorporated into the aircraft.
