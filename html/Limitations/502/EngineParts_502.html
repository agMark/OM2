The Pratt & Whitney PT6A engine has several life limited components. Review the applicable engine maintenance manual and service bulletins for the specific engine model to determine the life limited components and their limits.

Engine Model Service Bulletin(s)
PT6A-15AG SB12102
PT6A-34, -36, -34B SB1002
PT6A-34AG SB1302
PT6A-60AG SB13202
PT6A-65AG 13202
PT6A-65B 13002
PT6A-140AG see EMM 3079582, Table 1

As outlined in these service bulletins, operators are required to keep records of accumulated total cycles and all starts and flights for their engines to determine when the service life of life limited components has expired.
