The Pratt & Whitney PT6A engine has several life limited components. P&W Service Bulletin 3002 Revision No. 19, or later revision, describes the life limited components, and their limits for the PT6A-45 series engines. Service Bulletin 13002 Revision No. 9, or later revision, describes the life limited components, and their limits for the PT6A-65B, PT6A-65R and PT6A-65AR series engines. Service Bulletin 13202 describes the life limited components, and their limits for the PT6A-65AG series engines. Service Bulletin 14502, describes the life limited components, and their limits for the PT6A-67AG and PT6A-67F series engines.

As outlined in these service bulletins, operators are required to keep records of accumulated total cycles and all starts and flights for their engines to determine when the service life of life limited components has expired.
