Annual Inspections

In the United States, annual inspections are required to be performed per 14 CFR 91.409(a)(1). The scope of the annual inspection must comply with Appendix D to 14 CFR Part 43.

Operators outside the United States must ensure that their inspection program complies with the requirements of the applicable aviation authority.

Manufacturer's Recommended Inspection Program

This manufacturer inspection program shown in the remainder of this section provides for the complete inspection of the aircraft each 12 calendar months.

Recurring maintenance and inspections published in service letters and service information letters have been incorporated into this manual. This means that this manual provides a complete listing of the recurring airframe maintenance that Air Tractor recommends (not necessarily one-time actions). You should check if service letters have been published after those incorporated into this manual to determine if additional compliance actions are necessary. All months referenced in this section are “calendar" months. (For example: If the 12-month inspection was completed on January 10, 2025, the next 12-month inspection is due no later than January 31, 2026).

Hourly Inspections and Interval Tolerance - The 100-, 200-, and 300-hour inspections should be performed at the intervals shown, within a tolerance window of +/- 15 hours (if they are not combined with the 12-month inspection). Do not add or subtract the times within this tolerance to the next scheduled inspection. (For example: If the 100-hour inspection item was due at 5400 hours, and the inspection was performed at 5385-5415 hours, the next 100-hour inspection item remains due at 5500 hours). Inspections performed outside of this tolerance will require a new next-due calculation.

For all other applicable intervals (400, 800, 900, 1000, 1350, 2000, 5000 etc.), the interval shown is the maximum (no +/- 15-hour tolerance).
