The elevator trim tab system uses the elevator tabs as both trim tabs and as elevator boost tabs. The boost tabs function to relieve a portion of the control load during maneuvers.

Elevator trim is controlled by the quadrastat lever on the left-hand wall of the cockpit. The quadrastat serves to keep the trim control lever in a fixed position unless adjusted by the pilot.

A bellcrank is attached to the fuselage frame with AN bolts, Bunting bushings, spacer bushings and AN nuts and washers under the nuts. The bellcrank should be removed during 12-month inspections and the bushings inspected for wear and lubricated.

The pushrods from the trim bellcrank to the idler arm on the stabilizer rear spar are adjustable. The ends are AN clevis ends with AN check nuts. Attachments are with AN bolts, nuts and washers and Mil Spec cotter pins. The long pushrod going forward from the bellcrank has a Fafnir bearing attached to the bellcrank with a AN bolt, washers and a nut.

Fairleads have snap rings and the forward end going into the cockpit connects to the top hole in the trim lever with the same hardware as connecting to the trim bellcrank.

Two pushrods attach to the trim tab horn with AN bolts and nuts and attaches to the idler arm in the stabilizer with AN bolts, nuts, washers and Mil Spec cotter pins. Be sure the pushrod is positioned with the bend on the forward end approximately under the elevator spar tube. There is a Bunting bushing at the forward joint which should be replaced if the tab free play is excessive. If excessive free play still exists, check the bolts and pushrod ends for wear. Install new pushdrods if the holes are worn.

Tab Travel

Tab travel is checked with the elevator in the nuetral position. From this point, the trim tab should move as shown below.

Model Up Limit Down Limit
AT-502A 9° ± 1.5° 7° ± 1.5°
AT-502B 9° ± 1.5° 7° ± 1.5°
AT-504 9° ± 1.5° 7° ± 1.5°
AT-501* 9° ± 1.5° 9° ± 1.5°

Note: 501 control travel provided here for convenience.

The stop plate is in quadrastat mechanism. Start with the plate flush with the bottom of the faceplate then adjust vertically as required. If rodends are adjusted, make sure to tighten all checknuts after adjustment.

Maximum Tab Free Play

In order to avoid the possibility of control surface flutter, the tab must not have excessive free play.

Maximum free play for the elevator trim tab (which includes all bearing free play in the control system) is .020 in. To determine free play, use a dial indicator to measure the movement of the trim tab trailing edge.

Quadrastat Maintenance

The quadrastat mechanism should be clean and free of dirt and corrosion. Do not grease the quadrastat because it relies on friction to operate.
