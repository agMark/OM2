Details of the elevator trim tab controls are shown in ref The elevator trim tab system uses the elevator tabs as both trim tabs and as elevator boost tabs. The boost tabs function to relieve a portion of the control load during maneuvers.

Elevator trim is controlled by the quadrastat lever on the left-hand wall of the cockpit. The quadrastat serves to keep the trim control lever in a fixed position unless adjusted by the pilot.

A pushrod connects the elevator trim quadrisat in the cockpit to the elevator trim bellcrank in the aft fuselage. The forward end of the pushrod is connected to the bottom hole in the trim lever on the quadrastat. The pushrod has adjustable Fafnir bearings at each end. Fairleads support the pushrod along the length of the fuselage and have snap rings to keep them in place.

The elevator trim bellcrank is attached to the aft fuselage frame with AN bolts, Bunting bushings, spacer bushings and AN nuts and washers under the nuts. The bellcrank should be removed during 12-month inspections and the bushings inspected for wear and lubricated.

Each trim tab horn is connected to each idler arm with a pushrod. Be sure the pushrod is positioned with the bend on the forward end approximately under the elevator spar tube. There is a Bunting bushing at the forward joint which should be replaced if the tab free play is excessive. If excessive free play exists after replacing the bushings, check the bolts and pushrod ends for wear. Install new pushdrods if the holes are worn.

Maximum free play for the elevator trim tab (which includes all bearing free play in the control system) is 0.20 in. To determine free play, use a dial indicator to measure the movement of the trim tab trailing edge.

Tab Travel

Tab travel is checked with the elevator in the neutral position. The stop plate is in quadrastat mechanism. Start with the plate flush with the bottom of the faceplate then adjust vertically as required. The stop should be adjusted to provide a tab travel of 9°±1.5° up, and 7°±1.5° down. If rod ends are adjusted, make sure to tighten all checknuts after adjustment.

Quadrastat Maintenance

The quadrastat mechanism should be clean and free of dirt and corrosion. Do not grease the quadrastat because it relies on friction to operate.
