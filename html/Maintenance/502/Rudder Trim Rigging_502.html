The rudder boost tab should initially be set so that the tab is centered on the undeflected rudder while the trim wheel is centered on the "TAKEOFF" setting. This can be done by adjusting the pushrod connected to the tab control horn. After initial rigging, the airplane should be flown and the pushrod adjusted as necessary. Lengthening the pushrod will move the nose to the right. Shortening the pushrod will move the nose to the left. A part number 70954-6 pushrod may be used if a shorter pushrod is required for propper rigging. Verify adequate thread engagement for all pushrods and hardware.
