Gaskets for both should be replaced when either is removed. Original and new gaskets should not be mixed when replacing. Gaskets for the adapter to gate box connection are available from Transland or Air Tractor. Bolts for attaching the hopper to the gatebox are stainless steel. These bolts are installed with stainless AN washers and Mil Spec nuts. Gaskets should be sealed on both sides. Bolt torque is 150 inch-pounds.

The gate box may be removed by use of the following procedure:

  1. Remove fuselage side skins adjacent to the hopper tank.
  2. Remove the gate control rod attachments to the gate box.
  3. Remove attachments to spray pump line.
  4. remove the bottom-load fill attachments to the hopper.
  5. Remove the bolts that attach the gate box to the hopper and lower the gate box gently to the ground.
  6. Remove the hose couplings between the hopper vent outlets and the vent stacks inside and on both sides of the hopper adapter.

The gate box may be replaced by reversing the removal procedure. Clean surfaces of all old sealant. Wipe down surfaces with solvent to remove all residues. Install gatebox with new gaskets and sealant.
