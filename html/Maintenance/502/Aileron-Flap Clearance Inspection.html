This inspection will consist of checking for proper rigging/ functioning of the flap system and checking for any possible interference issues that may exist. Please use the following steps to complete this inspection. Repeat all steps for both the left and right sides.

  1. Remove the lower side skins at the wing flap area to allow access to the flap system.
  2. With the flaps in the retracted position:
    1. Inspect the flap pushrod and the aileron pushrod for any paint scuffs, damage, or evidence of part-to-part contact.
    2. Inspect that the pushrods have at least 3/16” between them. See
    3. Inspect to ensure that the aileron pushrod bolts are oriented fore-and-aft in the pushrod and not up-and-down. (Note: all aircraft come from the factory with the bolts oriented fore-and-aft. Check these bolts to ensure this hasn’t changed due to repair or alteration.)
    4. Check both flaps to ensure that the leading edge of the flap is not touching the flap bay skin of the wing. This would indicate the upper limit switch setting may be wrong or that the flap is coasting past the limit switch.
    5. Move the aileron controls through their full range of motion slowly while having someone watch the clearance between the aileron controls and the flap pushrod. The clearance should remain at least 3/16” throughout all motions of the aileron controls.
  3. Hold the FLAPS switch in the DOWN position while moving the aileron controls from stop-to-stop while having someone watch the clearance between the aileron controls and the flap pushrod. At no point should the clearance be less than 3/16”.
  4. Hold the FLAPS switch in the UP position. Approximately half way through the flap travel, release the switch. The flaps should stop moving instantly and should not “coast to a stop”. (Note: When properly wired, the flap system electrically connects the two leads of the flap actuator motor. This acts as an electric brake to stop the flaps instantly and hold them in the desired position. If the incorrect toggle switch is installed or the system is not wired per factory specs, then the electric braking action will not work. Do not install an automotive toggle switch; it will not work as designed.)
  5. Move the flaps down using the FLAPS switch. Before the flaps are fully extended, release the switch. Again the flaps should stop moving instantly and should not “coast to a stop”.


  6. Hold the FLAPS switch in the DOWN position. Ensure the down limit switch engages to stop the flap travel at full deflection.
  7. With the flaps in the down position, look at the flap bay skin on the wing and the flap leading edge to ensure there is no evidence of these two parts rubbing.
  8. Return the flaps to the retracted position using the FLAPS switch.
  9. With the flaps up, check the gap between the flap actuator ball nut and the gearbox. This gap should be between 1/8” and 3/8”. See Figure 3.
