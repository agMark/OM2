Attachment Bolts

Horizontal stabilizers are attached with two NAS bolts which have a washer under the head and two under the nut as shown in and . Torque is 100 inch-pounds on the nut.

The gap cover should be removed from the stabilizer and the bolt condition and torque checked during 12-month inspections. Inspect the fuselage fittings closely for cracks at the same time.

Eyebolts

Two eyebolts are installed inside the stabilizer for attachment of the struts. The eyebolts are AN47-22A parts and use two MS20002-7 washers and an AN365-720A nut.

Replacement intervals for these eyebolts are provided in Section 2.200.1.

Gap Covers

The gap covers are attached with stainless self-tapping screws which are Phillips truss head. A No. 39 hole is drilled for the screw installation. The rubber channel is attached with 3M adhesive.

Trim Controls

There are a left hand and right hand trim control idler attached to each stabilizer rear spar on the inboard end. Each trim idler has two Bunting bushings that are press fit inside the welded assembly with a spacer bushing allowing the AN attach bolt to be fully torqued (35 inch-pounds at the nut). The brackets that attach the trim idlers should never need replacing unless the AN bolt becomes loose and causes wear in the holes.

One of the idler arms is attached to the pushrod connected to the trim tab horn and this arm has a Bunting bushing press-fit into the idler arm. This is the same size bushing in the trim tab horn and both should be replaced when excessive trim tab free play develops. The Bunting bushings are impregnated with oil. If new ones are installed, fill the bushings to overflowing with general purpose oil and apply finger pressure on the ends to force the oil into the small cavities inside the bushings. The bushing connections should be oiled regularly.

Abrasion Tape

3M anti-abrasion tape is installed on the leading edge of the stabilizer. This tape should be periodically checked for condition and replaced as required. It is permissible to operate without the tape but expect paint quality and life to diminish.
