Spray lever controls vary between AT-502/AT-502A/AT-502B and AT-504. Each is detailed here.

AT-502/AT-502A/AT-502B

Details of the spray-lever controls are shown below in Fig Cross Reference. The p/n 80073-1 spray lever assembly is attached to the aft cockpit wall using AN4-5A bolts and AN960-416L washers and has an AG-235 micro-control attached to limit the lever travel and hence the spray pressure. Keep the threaded screw on the micro-control greased and make sure the tab on the spray lever contacts the bushing that is attached to the lower adjustment block.

The push-rod from the spray lever is p/n 80063-2 and has an adjustable Fafnir REP4M6 bearing in the lower end and a Fafnir REP4H6 bearing in the upper end. The pushrod is attached to the bellcrank with an AN4-12A bolt.

The lower bellcrank is attached to the fuselage with an AN4-25A bolt. The bellcrank has two Fafnir KP6A bearings with a p/n 70081-2 spacer bushing that allows the bolt to be fully torqued.

The bellcrank is p/n 80068-1 and passes through a p/n 80132-1 boot and p/n 70140-1 boot ring attached to the lower fuselage skin with 90013-4 screws and MS21044C08 nuts. The push-rod from the bellcrank to the valve is p/n 80065-1 which has an NMB MS14104-4 bearing in each end.

AT-504

Details of the spray-lever controls are shown in Fig Cross Reference. The Transland p/n 21613 spray lever assembly is attached to the aft cockpit wall using AN4-13A bolts and passes through p/n 83421-1 boot secured with p/n 83420-1 boot ring and p/n 90008-1 screws. The spray lever uses an adjustable micro-control built-in to the spray handle to limit the lever travel and hence the spray pressure. Keep the threaded ajustment rod located on the underside of the spray handle greased to ensure easy operation.

The push rod from the spray lever is p/n 83408-1 and has an adjustable Fafnir REP4M6 bearing in the lower end and a Fafnir REP4H6 bearing in the upper end.

The lower bellcrank is p/n 83407-1 and is attached to the fuselage frame with two AN4-10A bolts. The bellcrank has two MS14104-4 bearings and passes through p/n 80132-1 boot and p/n 70140-1 boot ring attached to the lower fuselage skin with 90013-4 screws and MS21044C08 nuts.

The pushrod from the bellcrank to the valve is p/n 80065-8 which has a NMB MS14104-4 bearing in each end.

The spray lever in the cockpit can be adjusted to personal preference by twisting the handle clockwise or counterclockwise as shown in Fig Cross Reference . The procedures that may be followed for adjustment of the spray-lever position in the cockpit:

  1. Remove the bolt that secures the adjustment bearing to the bellcrank.
  2. Loosen the check nut on the stem of the clevis.
  3. Secure the bellcrank to hold the spray valve in the SPRAY OFF position.
  4. Secure the cockpit spray handle in the desired position for SPRAY OFF.
  5. Adjust the clevis to align with the bolt holes in the bellcrank.
  6. Replace the bolt, washer (s), and nut and retighten the check nut.
  7. Check travel of spray handle in cockpit. If necessary, repeat steps 1 through 6 for best fit.
