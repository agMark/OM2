The propeller overspeed governor serves to limit propeller rpm in the event of a failure of the primary governor.

If the overspeed governor is out of adjustment such that it governs below the primary governor, the propeller may appear to surge. If this occurs, check and overhaul of the overspeed governor is likely required.

Issues on Certain Early 502XP Aircraft

AT-502A aircraft fitted with the PT6A-140AG engine and four bladed Hartzell HC-B4TN-3C propellers (branded the AT-502XP) were originally approved with a Woodward P/N: A210507 Overspeed Governor installed. It has been shown that the max RPM set speed of this governor is slightly too low and may interfere with normal operation of the primary governor, causing propeller surging at takeoff power settings. Applicable aircraft serial numbers 502A-3076 and prior with PT6A-140AG engines installed (also known as “502XP” aircraft) are part of this group. Refer to Service Letter #360 to correct this concern.
