Spray nozzles are standard from the factory. The factory offers several different spray nozzle configurations, including standard brass nozzles, and CP Nozzle products. Spray booms setup should be tailord for appliacton type and rates always utilizing check valves. After any adjustment to quantity of nozzles or tip sizes, spray pattern testing should be performed.

Be sure to apply 3M Permatex or equivalent to the threads of the hex nipples or plugs during installation.
