Rigging Instructions:

Complete these instructions on both sides of the aircraft simultaneously.

  1. Ensure that the aileron and rudder controls have been rigged per the instructions in ref and ref.
  2. Unlock the control stick.
  3. Manually center the rudder and hold in place.
  4. Visually ensure that the rudder pedals are even in the cockpit. Adjust if needed.
  5. Visually ensure that the control stick is centered (left-right) in the cockpit. If the control stick is not centered, tighten the turnbuckle shown in Fig Cross Reference for the aileron bellcrank on the side the control stick is deflecting toward. The opposite turnbuckle may also be loosened.
  6. Move the control stick to to one side and release it. The control stick should move back to center. If the control stick does not center, the turnbuckles can be tightened so that the stick will center when released.
  7. Make sure that the turnbuckles have sufficient thread engagement and safety.
  8. Check the cable routing for pulleys that are out of alignment and for cable contact with any structure. Check the cables for any damage or wear that would necessitate replacement (Reference: FAA Advisory Circular 43.13-1B).
  9. Ensure that all hardware is tight and properly installed.
  10. Unlock the controls and move the rudder pedals and aileron controls through their full range of motion. Check for any binding or sticking of controls. Applying full right-hand rudder input should result in a tendency for the right-hand aileron to rise and applying full left-hand rudder input should result in a tendency for the left-hand aileron to rise.

Air Tractor has received a report of an in-flight jamming of a rudder pedal. The pilot was able to maintain control and, after landing, a cable pulley in the rudder system was found to be broken. Due to the numerous unsecured items in the baggage compartment, it is believed that one or more items fouled the cables causing the cables to jam resulting in a subsequent failure of the pulley.
