AT-502A/502B

Details of the AT-502A/502B rudder controls are shown in .

The rudder cables attach to stainless link plates at the rudder horn with AN bolts, nuts, washers and Mil Spec cotter pins. There are four pulleys in the rudder cable system to the cockpit.

In the cockpit, the rudder cables attach to a steel plate which is bolted to an adjustable aluminum channel. Also attached to each plate is an aileron-rudder interconnect cable assembly. Each interconnect cable assembly attaches to the interconnect spring below the cockpit which attaches to the aileron 180° bellcrank on the opposite side of the airplane.

The rudder pedal hanger assembly slides into the adjustable channel and is held in place with a Faspin. The hanger assembly has a spring attached to pull it forward to remove slack from the rudder cable. The spring is attached to an eyebolt in the hanger assembly. The rudder pedal castings may be removed by taking out the AN bolt through the Caplug on the inboard end of the hanger assembly and removing the AN bolt attaching the master cylinder strap.

The four (two per side) castle nuts used on the stainless link plates should be hand tightened, then backed off to the closest castillation before installing the cotter.

AT-504

Details of the AT-504 rudder controls are shown in .

The rudder cables attach to stainless link plates at the rudder horn with AN bolts, nuts, washers and Mil Spec cotter pins. There are two pulleys in the rudder cable system to the rudder pedal interconnect bellcranks.

Behind the cockpit, the rudder cables attach to the outboard arm of each rudder pedal interconnect bellcrank. Also attached to the outboard arm of each bellcrank is an aileron-rudder interconnect cable assembly. Each interconnect cable assembly attaches to the interconnect spring below the cockpit which attaches to the aileron 180° bellcrank on the opposite side of the airplane.

The rudder pedal interconnect bellcranks are connected to each other through a pushrod. The bellcranks are connected to the aluminum rudder pedal adjustment channels. The rudder pedal hanger assembly slides into the adjustable channel and is held in place with a Faspin. The rudder pedal castings may be removed by taking out the AN bolt through the Caplug on the outboard end of the hanger assembly and removing the AN bolt attaching the master cylinder strap.

The four (two per side) castle nuts used on the stainless link plates should be hand tightened, then backed off to the closest castillation before installing the cotter.

Rudder Pedal Inspection

Remove castings and inspect for cracks around the bronze bushing, bushing wear and general condition. Grease the hanger assembly and slide the casting back in place. Be sure to use the AN castle nut with an Mil Spec cotter pin on the AN bolt connecting the master cylinder strap. Also, be sure two AN washers are installed between the strap and casting and work the brake several times to be sure clearance exists between the strap and the casting. While the casting is removed, check the condition of the bronze bushing in the master cylinder strap. Oil the bronze bushings with general purpose oil before re-installing the bolts. The two (four for 504) castle nuts used to attach the master cylinder straps to the rudder pedal castings should assembled by hand tightening the nut, then backing the nut off to the nearest castillation before installing the cotter.
