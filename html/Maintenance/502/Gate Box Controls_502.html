Details of the gatebox controls are shown below in ref. The long pushrod assembly attached to the over-center controls of the gatebox has a Mil Spec bearing in the aft end and a Bunting bronze bushing in the forward end. The aft end connects to the torque tube assembly and is supported on the fuselage lower longerons with two Fafnir bearings attached with AN bolts.

The boot is attached to the lower fuselage skin with a boot ring, screws and Mil Spec nuts. A pushrod connects the lower torque tube to the hopper handle in the cockpit and has adjustable Fafnir bearings on both ends. The hopper handle is also supported on each end with Fafnir bearings with AN bolts inserted through the bearings.
