The airplane equipped with a rudder boost tab (trim tab on the AT-502A) that reduces pedal forces in normal flight. The tab is made of a single skin wrapped around a welded spar and rib frame. The tab is controlled by a single pushrod on its right-hand side. This pushrod has threaded studs on both sides to attach the two rodend bearings. The tab installation is shown below in Fig Cross Reference.

The tab is held in place by two studs that fit loosely into pre-oiled porous bronze bearings in each end of the tab spar. The upper stud is fixed to the rudder, and the lower stud is attached to the end of a metal strap that is itself bolted to the bottom-aft of the rudder.

The tab may be removed by first removing the hardware attaching the control horn to the pushrod. The tab can be separated from the rudder by removing the two AN bolts that fasten the strap and stud to the bottom of the rudder. The lower stud can be pulled free, and the tab can be removed.
