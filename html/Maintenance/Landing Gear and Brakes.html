Ensure to take proper precautions when performing maintenance on landing gear there are several hazards.

Air Tractor suggests consideration of the following:

  • Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE).
  • Allow tires and brakes to cool before maintenance and handling.
  • Completely deflate tire before removal.
  • Do not loosen bolts or nuts while the tire is inflated.
  • Use an inflation containment / safety cage.
  • Do not exceed rated inflation pressures.
