The wiring schematic for the stall warning horn is shown in Fig Cross Reference below. The horn is connected in series with the lift-detector switch that is located near the leading edge of a mid-span of the left-hand wing.

The lift-detector switch is controlled by a tap that projects forward into the airstream. When at rest, the weight of the tab holds it down, opening the switch. When the airflow around the lift detector shifts to a forward direction, that tab is lifted, closing the switch and causing the horn to sound.

To adjust the stall warning horn, loosen the screws and move the lift detector up or down and re-tighten screws. A test flight must be performed to verify that the horn sounds at approximately 5 mph above stall speed at maximum takeoff weight.
