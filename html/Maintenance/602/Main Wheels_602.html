For instructions concerning maintenance and overhaul of the main wheels, refer to Cleveland (Parker) Document AWBCMM0001-(current revision).

To remove a main wheel, jack the wheel clear of the ground per . See Fig Cross Reference below for details of the main wheels. Remove the six bolts attaching the two halves of each brake assembly. Leave the brake line connected and slide the brake assembly out of the way. Remove the outer snap ring, dust cap, two axle nut cotter pins and finally the castellated axle nut. The wheel may then be pulled free of the axle.

If the tire is to be removed, completely deflate the tire by removing the air valve stem Schrader valve. Remove the bolts holding the two wheel halves together. Torque wheel bolts 150 inch-pounds at the nut. Inflate to 50 (unloaded) or 52 (loaded) psi. Clean bearings and hand pack with grease. Before the wheel is reinstalled, inspect the cast aluminum torque plate for cracks and check the five axle attach bolts for proper torque (800 inch-pounds at the nut). Wipe axle clean and slide inner grease seal and bearing on axle. Position wheel and tighten castellated axle nut until a slight bearing drag is felt when wheel is rotated. Back off nut to nearest castellation and install two MS24665-351 cotter pins. Install snap ring and outer dust cap. The cotter pin heads should protrude no more than 1/8" above the flat for dust cap clearance. Check brake linings for wear and reinstall on torque plate. The six bolts in each wheel cylinder require 80 to 90 inch-pounds of torque. Secure the bolts with MS20995C32 safety wire.
