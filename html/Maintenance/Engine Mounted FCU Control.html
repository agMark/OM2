Airplanes Without Cockpit FCU Override Lever

Airplanes that are equipped with an FCU Override Lever on the engine but are not equipped with an FCU Override Lever in the cockpit will need to have the engine FCU override lever safety wired in the closed position similar to what is shown below. If the FCU Override lever on the engine is not safetied in the closed position, the lever could inadvertently move toward open and cause engine damage.
