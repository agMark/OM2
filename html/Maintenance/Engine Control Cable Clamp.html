We have received reports of cables slipping within the 70462-1/-2 clamps. Typically this is a result of improper torqueing procedure.

The inner bolts must be torqued before the outer bolts. See ref

Beginning in 2026, new aircraft are equipped with 71663-1/-2 clamps which feature an improved retention design. If you have issues with cable slippage, we suggest that you upgrade to the new clamp design.
