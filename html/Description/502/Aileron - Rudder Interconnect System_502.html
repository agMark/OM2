The aileron-rudder interconnect system assists with turn coordination when the rudder or ailerons are used.

A stainless-steel cable attached to the rudder pedals is routed through a pulley system below the cockpit to the 180° aileron bellcrank. The cable has a spring attached to the end below the cockpit which applies a force to the aileron controls as the rudder controls are manipulated. The interconnect system will also apply control forces to the rudder pedals when the aileron controls are manipulated.
