The propeller used on the AT-602 equipped with PT6A-45R/-45A/-45B/-60AG/-65AR/-65B/-65R/-65AG engines is a Hartzell HC-B5MP-3C/M10876AS or /M10876ANS five-bladed unit 111.2" in diameter. The pitch settings of this propeller are -11.0° (reverse), 16.5° (low pitch) and 79.0° (feather) at the 42" station.

AT-602s equipped with PT6A-65AR/-65B/-65R/-65AG engines may also use a Hartzell HC-B5MP-3F/M11276NS five bladed unit 115.2" in diameter. The pitch settings of this propeller are -10.0° (reverse), 13.9° (low pitch) and 83.1° (feather) at the 42" station.

The primary prop governor is manufactured by Woodward and furnished with the engine. The overspeed governor takes over in case of primary governor failure, automatically reducing propeller speed 30-60 RPM below the maximum propeller RPM.
