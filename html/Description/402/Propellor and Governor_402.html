The propeller used on the AT-402/AT-402A/AT-402B is a Hartzell HC-B3TN-3D/T10282+4 or /T10282N+4 or /T10282NS+4 three-bladed 106.0" diameter constant speed unit with reversing capabilities. Propeller pitch settings are -8.0° (reverse), 18.0° (low pitch) and 86.0-88.0° (feather) at the 30" station.

The primary prop governor is manufactured by Woodward and furnished with the engine. The overspeed governor takes over in case of primary governor failure, automatically reducing propeller speed 30-60 RPM below the maximum propeller RPM.
